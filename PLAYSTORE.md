# Play Store Submission Package — LectorMobile

## App Store Listing

### Short Description (80 chars)
PDF & EPUB reader with dark mode, bookmarks, fast & minimal

### Full Description (4000 chars)
**LectorMobile** is the simplest, fastest PDF and EPUB reader for Android. Built for readers who value their time and want a distraction-free reading experience.

## ✨ Why LectorMobile?

Most reading apps are bloated with ads, accounts, and features you never use. LectorMobile is different:

- **No Ads** — Ever. Clean, distraction-free reading.
- **No Accounts** — No sign-up, no registration, no tracking.
- **No Internet Needed** — Your books stay on your device.
- **True Dark Mode** — Not just a theme — optimized for night reading.
- **Lightweight & offline** — Fast to install, no network required.

## 📖 Features

### PDF Reading
- Full PDF document support
- Pinch-to-zoom and swipe navigation
- Page slider to jump anywhere in the document
- Remembers the last page you read

### EPUB Reading
- EPUB 2.0 and EPUB 3.0 support
- Table of contents (nav.xhtml and toc.ncx)
- Chapter-by-chapter reading in spine order
- Customizable font size
- Dark reading background with light text

### Bookmarks & Progress
- Save reading position automatically
- Manual bookmark creation
- Manage all bookmarks in one place
- Resume reading where you left off

### Design
- Material Design 3 interface
- Dark theme by default
- Clean, minimal UI
- Smooth animations

## 🔒 Privacy

LectorMobile respects your privacy:

- No internet permissions needed
- No data collection
- No tracking
- No ads
- All processing happens locally on your device

## 📱 System Requirements

- Android 7.0 (API 24) or higher
